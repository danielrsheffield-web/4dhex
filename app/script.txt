Hover over a hex. It will display all hexes adjacent to it. In 2d hex, each hex has 6 neighbors (unless it's on the edge). In 4d hex, each hex has up to 30 neighbors, 6 on its own board, and 4 on each board adjacent to that board. Only the very middle cell of the 3x3x3x3 actually has all thirty neighbors, as every other cell is on the edge in one way or another. The basic rule is that a hex is adjacent to another if you can get from one to another by increasing some coordinates by 1 and leaving others alone, or by decreasing some coordinates by 1 and leaving others alone, but not mixing increasing and decreasing coordinates.

In 4d hex, the goal is to create a surface around the edges of the board. Each player is trying to form a surface along different dimensions, and it's impossible for them both to form surfaces. This tutorial will primarily focus on what constitues a surface, as it can be counterintuitive.
